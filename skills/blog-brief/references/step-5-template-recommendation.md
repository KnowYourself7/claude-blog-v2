### Step 5: Template Recommendation

#### Goal

Choose one of the 12 content templates for this post.

#### Inputs

- `briefs/[slug]-search-intent.md`: the reader task.
- `briefs/[slug]-competitor-[n].md`: the format of each ranking page,
  in its Structure section.
- `../blog/references/content-templates.md`: the 12 templates and
  what each is for.

#### Rules

- The ranking pages show the format that works. Choose from them, not
  from the demand facets or from the words of a title.

#### Steps

1. Read the reader task.
2. Read the Structure section of each competitor. Note the format that
   the ranking pages share.
3. Choose the template that fits that format and the reader task.
   Write the reason in one sentence.
4. Read `../blog/templates/[type].md`.

#### Outputs

- No file. The template name and the reason go into the Template
  section of the brief in Step 9.

#### Check

- One template is chosen.
- The reason names the format of the ranking pages.
