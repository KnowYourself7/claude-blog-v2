### Step 1: Topic Intake

Read `templates/task-brief.md` first. Fill it from what the user
wrote, and save it as `briefs/[slug]-task-brief.md`.

1. Topic: the focus keyword, the market, and the decision that the
   reader is making. If the user names no market, use 2840 / en and
   write `default`. You can infer the reader decision from the
   keyword.
2. Business: the brand, the domain, what it sells, and what it does
   not sell or must not claim.
3. Page: the page that this post supports, and the next step for the
   reader.

Write `missing` in every cell that the user did not give. Do not open
the website. Do not infer a brand fact. Do not ask for the search
intent. Step 2 reads it from the live SERP.
