### Step 6: Statistics Research

#### Goal

Find the statistics that the article needs, with enough provenance to
verify each one.

#### Inputs

- None. The topic is the focus keyword of the task brief.

#### Rules

- Prioritize tier 1-3 sources.
- Drop unverifiable statistics instead of carrying them as weak
  claims.

#### Steps

1. Search: `[topic] study 2025 2026 data statistics research`. A pool
   of about 8-12 candidates is a reasonable start; keep only those you
   can verify and that the article needs.
2. For each statistic, record enough provenance to verify it:
   publisher and title, URL, relevant date or study period, and
   methodology or retrieval date when those details affect
   interpretation.
3. Note the statistics that a chart explains better than prose, for
   the Visual Element Plan of the brief.
4. Note 1-2 statistics for the TL;DR and for social sharing.

#### Outputs

- No file. The statistics go into the Statistics to Include table of
  the brief in Step 7.

#### Check

- Every kept statistic has a publisher, a URL, and a date.
- No unverified statistic is kept.
